    About Us

    Empowering Children with Learning Disabilities: The I CAN SPECIAL NEEDS FOUNDATION

    Our Mission

    I CAN SPECIAL NEEDS FOUNDATION is a faith-based charity dedicated to supporting children with learning disabilities and their families in Nigeria. With years of experience in special education and community support, our foundation is committed to providing tailored interventions that foster growth, learning, and independence. Our team includes special education teachers, therapists, and counsellors who understand the unique challenges faced by children with learning disabilities.

    Our Unique Approach

    1

    What sets I CAN SPECIAL NEEDS FOUNDATION apart is our believe that every child can thrive with the right support whilst within a compassionate and supportive community. Our foundation fosters hope and resilience, empowering children and families to overcome obstacles and achieve their fullest potential.

    2

    I CAN supports children with special needs in Nigeria, mainly from early years to age sixteen. But our work goes beyond the child—we focus on the environment around the child

    3

    Our work focuses on early identification, supporting communication, and managing behaviour with empathy, not punishment. We also support families, walking alongside parents with understanding and encouraging early intervention.

    Team

    Titilola Adenuga

    Founder & CEO

    Titilola Adenuga is the Founder of I CAN SPECIAL NEEDS FOUNDATION, with over ten years of experience in the special needs sector, she has been a passionate advocate for inclusive education, early intervention, and equitable access to support services.

    Driven by a deep commitment to improving outcomes for families, Titilola established the foundation to address gaps in provision and create meaningful support systems for children with diverse learning needs.

    Oluwaseun Osindero

    Programme Director

    Oluwaseun is an experienced Programme Director with over ten years of leadership within Special Educational Needs (SEN) and inclusive education. She has led complex, multi-agency programmes focused on improving outcomes for children and young people with diverse learning needs, including autism, speech and language needs, social, emotional and mental health (SEMH), and profound and multiple learning difficulties..

    Veronica Adewumi

    Marketing and Communication Officer

    Veronica Adewumi is an entrepreneur, enthusiast brand communicator and a customer relations professional with over 25 years of experience managing clients relationships and working closely with individuals from diverse backgrounds. She has developed strong expertise in brand communication within the printing industry, building meaningful connections and fostering lasting relationships with her many clients from different fields of life. 

    Beyond her professional pursuits, she is passionate about people and community impact, she is committed to supporting initiatives that empower individuals, strengthen communities and create lasting positive change in life.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does I CAN SPECIAL NEEDS FOUNDATION provide support?

    We provide support through educational programs, counseling, advocacy, and community outreach initiatives rooted in faith-based principles.

    Do you offer financial assistance or scholarships?

    Yes, we offer financial assistance and scholarships to eligible children and families to help access educational resources and therapies.

    Is the foundation open to collaborations with other organizations?

    Yes, we welcome collaborations with other organizations that share our vision and mission to improve the lives of children with learning disabilities.
